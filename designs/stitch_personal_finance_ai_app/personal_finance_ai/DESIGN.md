## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an authoritative, serene, and intelligent financial co-pilot. Designed for discerning individuals managing personal capital, portfolios, and household cash flow, the interface projects executive competence, total security, and analytical precision.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate with Editorial Restraint
The aesthetic fuses high-end Swiss structural order with refined digital utility:
- **Pristine Canvas Architecture**: Soft, ultra-light backdrops paired with crisp elevated operational cards to eliminate cognitive friction and eye fatigue during analytical reviews.
- **Deep Navy Command Framing**: Rich midnight tones ground the structural chrome, top-level navigation, and key conversion moments, establishing a secure banking-grade presence.
- **Intentional Semantic Chromaticism**: Vibrancy is reserved strictly for performance telemetry—emerald for growth and yields, crimson for outlays and liabilities, and warm amber for predictive budget alerts.
- **Deliberate Data Density**: Data points, tabular ledger rows, and numeric metrics follow strict tabular alignment, emphasizing readability and monetary legibility in Indian Rupees (`₹`).

## Colors

The palette balances deep naval governance with hyper-focused financial semantics against an airy, low-strain canvas.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#0A1628` - Obsidian Navy)**: The dominant structural color. Applied to headers, navigation chrome, high-priority primary actions, and key typographic metric anchors.
- **Secondary (`#16A34A` - Growth Emerald)**: Communicates incoming capital, positive delta, savings target achievements, and positive market positions.
- **Tertiary (`#DC2626` - Debit Crimson)**: Flags financial drains, outgoing capital, expense anomalies, and deficit alerts.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` - Slate Crisp)**: The default low-contrast base surface that prevents glare while sustaining deep contrast with cards.

### System Support Tokens
- **Alert / Notice (`#F59E0B` - Amber Alert)**: Reserved for budget warnings, approaching limits, pending verifications, and AI-detected reallocations.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White)**: Dedicated to elevated card containers, floating action surfaces, and active input fields.
- **Structural Border (`#E2E8F0` - Slate 200)**: Subtle perimeter boundaries for cards, dividers, modal headers, and table rows.
- **Text On Surface (`#0F172A` - Text Primary)**: Deep slate-charcoal for optimal optical contrast over white surfaces.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B` - Text Secondary)**: Mid-tone slate for supporting subtitles, transaction timestamps, and tabular labels.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes micro-legibility and geometric clarity across numerical values and complex ledger rows.

### Font Family & Features
- Primary Typeface: **Inter**
- Font Feature Settings: Enable tabular numbers (`tnum`) across all transaction lists, net worth indicators, balance figures, and charts to prevent jitter during dynamic updates. Enable slashed zeros (`zero`) where disambiguation is critical.

### Formatting Rules for Indian Currency (₹)
- The Rupee symbol (`₹`) is explicitly styled to inherit the font weight of its numerical sibling, paired without a trailing space (e.g., `₹1,24,500.00`).
- Use the Indian numbering format system (lakhs and crores: `1,00,000` and `1,00,00,000`) for localized user displays.
- Sub-paisa fractional precision is strictly restricted to secondary ledger records (`body-sm`), rendered in muted slate (`#64748B`).

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 4px/8px base spatial grid enforces systematic alignment and breathing room across dense financial dashboards.

### Mobile-First Layout Model
- **Mobile Grid**: 4 fluid columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` screen margin safe areas. Content cards span the full available grid width (4 columns).
- **Tablet Grid (600px - 1024px)**: 8 fluid columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins. Metric cards arrange in 2-column or 4-column balanced arrays.
- **Desktop/Expanded (> 1024px)**: 12 fluid columns constrained to a max-width of `1200px` centered canvas, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins.

### Component Spacing Discipline
- **Padding within Cards**: Standard financial cards utilize `1.25rem` (20px) horizontal and vertical inner padding to decouple borders from internal charts and balance summaries.
- **Stacking Metrics**: Metric labels maintain a strict `0.25rem` (`space-xs`) distance from primary numeric balance readouts.
- **List Item Separation**: Ledger and transaction rows maintain `0.75rem` vertical spacing, flanked by edge-to-edge subtle hairline dividing strokes.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established using layered planar surfaces and subtle, non-intrusive drop shadows rather than heavy skeuomorphic projections.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Tier (Ground Zero)**: `#F8FAFC`. Houses the ambient backdrop over which all modular intelligence panels sit.
2. **Structural Navigation**: `#0A1628`. High-contrast dark top and bottom bars that frame the light canvas, maintaining constant accessibility coordinates.
3. **Card Tier (Elevation 1)**: `#FFFFFF`. Elevated above the canvas with a twin-boundary structure:
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.02)`
4. **Floating Overlays & Modals (Elevation 2)**: Bottom sheets, action menus, and transactional review drawers:
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.03)`
5. **Interactive Focus / Tooltips (Elevation 3)**:
   - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(10, 22, 40, 0.04)`

## Shapes

The geometric identity utilizes deliberate corner radiuses, balancing welcoming organic softness with clean financial authority.

### Border Radius Rules
- **Core Cards & Panels**: Fixed at `16px` (`rounded-lg` under roundedness level 2). This defines all transaction clusters, metric snapshots, insights boxes, and balance containers.
- **Buttons & Interactive Controls**: `10px` to `12px` corner curvature, giving buttons a structured, clickable feel that avoids childlike pill morphology while remaining tactile.
- **Data Chips & Status Badges**: `6px` to `8px` subtle rounding to maintain high perimeter sharpness inside compact tables and list rows.
- **Floating Action Buttons (FAB) & Micro-Avatars**: Strict circular structures (`50%` radius) to cleanly differentiate operational triggers from functional data tiles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Navy (`#0A1628`) background with White (`#FFFFFF`) type, 12px border radius, 48px height for touch targets, medium font weight (`label-lg`). Pressed state dims to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary**: Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 1px solid border in Slate 200 (`#E2E8F0`), text in `#0A1628`.
- **Destructive**: Deep crimson tint background (`#FEF2F2`), border in `#FCA5A5`, text in `#DC2626`.
- **Semantic Action (Invest/Save)**: Emerald Green (`#16A34A`) fill with pure white label for clear call-to-action moments.

### Cards & Financial Containers
- **Base Financial Card**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 16px corner radius, hairline outline in `#E2E8F0`, Elevation 1 shadow.
- **Budget Alert Card**: Card surface `#FFFFFF` with a 4px left-border accent in Amber (`#F59E0B`), displaying warnings alongside actionable AI micro-copy.
- **Net Worth Summary Card**: Premium surface variation utilizing solid Navy (`#0A1628`) with White typography, Emerald (`#16A34A`) badge metrics, and high-opacity white secondary descriptions.

### Chips & Semantic Status Badges
- **Positive Gain / Credit**: Background `#DCFCE7` (Emerald 50), text `#16A34A`, label-md bold, prefixed with `+₹` or `↑`.
- **Expense / Debit**: Background `#FEE2E2` (Crimson 50), text `#DC2626`, label-md bold, prefixed with `-₹` or `↓`.
- **Warning / Alert**: Background `#FEF3C7` (Amber 50), text `#D97706`, label-md medium.
- **Category Filter Chip**: Unselected: `#FFFFFF` fill with `#E2E8F0` border; Selected: `#0A1628` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Text Input**: 48px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, 10px radius. Focus state swaps border to 2px solid `#0A1628`.
- **Rupee Currency Input**: Prominent display with an anchored leading `₹` glyph in `#64748B`. Font uses `metric-lg` with tabular formatting.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 20px dimension, 4px radius for checkboxes, 50% for radios. Border in `#CBD5E1`. Checked state fills with `#0A1628` displaying a white checkmark.

### Lists & Transaction Ledgers
- **Transaction Row**: Minimum 64px row height. Left: 40px rounded avatar/icon container with category-tinted backdrop; Center: Primary party name (`title-md`) stacked over timestamp and account label (`body-sm`); Right: Right-aligned currency metric (`metric-md`) styled in `#DC2626` for deductions or `#16A34A` for deposits.
- **Dividers**: 1px solid `#F1F5F9`, inset by 56px to align with text elements while bypassing avatar graphics.

### Specialized AI Insight Component
- **Recommendation Pill**: A container featuring a subtle gradient border transition from `#0A1628` to `#16A34A`, a micro-indicator spark icon, concise predictive copy in `body-sm`, and an immediate inline text CTA (e.g., "Optimize Portfolio").
